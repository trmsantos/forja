### Context
Forja was repurposed from an "AI services studio" marketing site into an **AR-collections
micro-SaaS**: it connects to a customer's Stripe (read-only) and auto-chases their overdue
invoices with escalating reminders. Target wedge: freelancers + small studios that invoice
via Stripe. Goal: €500+/month recurring, ≤10h/week supervision, must run if owner is offline
2 weeks. Pricing: €19 / €49 (featured) / €99 per month.
